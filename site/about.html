HELLO, WE’RE FANTASTIX

For curious minds
of every age.

Some of us grew up with a tiny Gaul taking on the world. Others found adventure in dusty frontier towns or on the far side of the globe. However you found comics, you belong here.

FANTASTIX brings those worlds together. We’re a fictional neighbourhood bookshop with a simple idea: a good story is even better when you can share it.

No algorithms. No endless scrolling. Just paper, pictures, and people who love a good recommendation.
